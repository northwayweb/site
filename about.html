About Northway Web

A Canadian web studio helping local businesses build a strong online presence.

Who We Are

Northway Web is a small, focused web studio based in Ontario, Canada. We specialize in building clean, modern websites for local service businesses that want to look professional and attract more customers online.

Many small businesses rely on word of mouth or outdated websites. We help bridge that gap by creating fast, mobile-friendly sites that make it easy for customers to contact you.

Why We Started

We started Northway Web after seeing how many great local businesses were losing opportunities simply because they didn’t have a proper website. Expensive agencies were out of reach, and DIY builders often created more frustration than results.

Our goal is simple: provide affordable, professional websites without unnecessary complexity or long timelines.

Who We Work With

We work with local service businesses across Ontario and Canada, including plumbers, electricians, HVAC companies, cleaning services, salons, auto repair shops, and other trades.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or updating an old site, we focus on clarity, speed, and results.

Our Approach

We keep things straightforward. No jargon. No bloated features. Just well-designed websites that load fast, look great on mobile, and help customers reach you easily.

Most projects are completed within a few days, and we’re always available to help as your business grows.
