About Northway Web

A Canadian web studio focused on helping local businesses grow online.

Who We Are

Northway Web is a small, focused web studio operating from Canada and working with businesses internationally. We build clean, professional websites for local service businesses that want to look credible and attract more customers online.

Why We Started

Many great local businesses lose opportunities simply because they do not have a clear, professional website. Large agencies are often expensive, and DIY builders rarely deliver real results.

We started Northway Web to offer a better option. Affordable websites built quickly, without unnecessary complexity.

Who We Work With

We work with local service businesses across North America and internationally, including plumbers, electricians, HVAC companies, cleaners, salons, auto repair shops, and other trades.

Whether you are starting from scratch or improving an outdated site, our focus is clarity, speed, and results.

Our Approach

We keep things simple. No jargon. No bloated features. Just fast-loading websites that look great on mobile and make it easy for customers to contact you.

Most projects are completed within a few days, and we provide clear communication from start to finish.
