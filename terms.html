Terms & Conditions

These terms outline our pricing, what’s included in your website build, and how we work together.

Overview

By working with Northway Web ("we", "us"), you ("client", "you") agree to the terms below. These terms are designed to keep expectations clear and the process smooth.

Pricing & Payment

Pricing is simple and transparent. Unless otherwise stated in writing, the following applies:

Starter Website: $399 (one-time payment)

Monthly Care & Hosting: $49/month (optional)

If you request additional pages, custom functionality, or special integrations, we will provide a quote before work begins.

What the One-Time Website Price Includes

The one-time website build is designed for local service businesses that need a fast, credible online presence. Your build typically includes:

  • One-page professional website (desktop + mobile responsive)
  • Clean design aligned with your business branding (colors, feel, layout)
  • Core sections such as services, about, trust signals, and contact
  • Click-to-call and contact form setup
  • Basic on-page SEO foundations (titles, descriptions, headings)
  • Performance-focused build (fast loading, simple structure)

If you want extras like more pages, booking systems, advanced SEO, or custom features, we’ll quote it separately.

Optional Monthly Care & Hosting

Monthly care is optional and can be cancelled at any time. It’s for clients who want us to handle hosting and ongoing upkeep. If you choose monthly care, it typically includes:

  • Secure hosting
  • Updates and backups
  • Basic content edits/support (reasonable small changes)

Timeline & Delivery

Typical delivery is within 72 hours once we receive the information needed to build your website. Delays can occur if key details or approvals are missing.

Client Responsibilities

To complete your project on time, you agree to provide the required information (business details, services, contact info, images/logos if available) and to respond to questions or requests for approval in a reasonable timeframe.

Revisions

Your build includes reasonable revisions to ensure the website matches your business and looks professional. Major redesign requests or scope changes may require an additional quote.

Refunds & Cancellations

Because websites are custom work, refunds are handled case-by-case. If a project is cancelled after work has started, completed work may be billed proportionally.

Results & Disclaimers

We build websites designed to improve credibility and make it easier for customers to contact you. However, we do not guarantee specific results such as rankings, traffic volume, or revenue.
